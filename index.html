Profile

I am an engineer working across robotics, computer control and mechatronics. I hold a robotics degree from the University of Illinois, have studied at the University of California, Berkeley, and have worked for globally recognised industrial technology companies, including Bosch, leading multidisciplinary teams to deliver complex projects.

I am now moving that experience into aviation: studying civil aviation operations and aviation theory, and flying both in aircraft and in the simulator. This site is where I keep the working record — requirements in plain English, the material I am studying, and a log of every session.
