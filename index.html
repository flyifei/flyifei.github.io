Cathay Pacific Cadet Pilot Programme — Preparation Notes

This site collects what I am learning on the way into the Cathay Pacific Cadet Pilot Programme: who is eligible and how to apply, how the airline and its training pathway work, the material I study, and a log of every session I fly — in the simulator and in a real aircraft.

Eligibility rules and process details change between recruitment cycles. Always confirm on Cathay careers before you invest months of preparation.
