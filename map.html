Where this comes from. Parsed from the simulator's own logbook file (sr_logbook, MSFS 2024 on Steam), which stores airport codes in a binary stream. Duplicated entries are collapsed and neighbouring airports counted as legs, so a leg marked “recorded once” may be an artefact of two sessions being adjacent in the file rather than a flight you actually made. Times show how often that leg appears in the log.

Dates, block times and landing counts are not stored as plain values in that file — they exist only in the simulator's logbook screen. If they matter, install a recorder such as Volanta or Little Navmap and the numbers become exportable.
