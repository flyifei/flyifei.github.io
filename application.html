Application

The Cadet Pilot Programme is the entry path that requires no previous flying hours: about 80 weeks of sponsored training, ending with a Commercial Pilot Licence and a Second Officer position in Hong Kong. Applications are open year-round.

Programme at a glance

DurationApproximately 80 weeks
Cost to the candidateSponsored training — the company funds the licence
Licence on completionCommercial Pilot Licence (CPL)
Flying experience requiredNone
ApplicationsOpen year-round
Training locationsGround theory in Hong Kong and overseas; flying training at international flight training organisations
First role afterwardsSecond Officer, based in Hong Kong

Eligibility

These are the requirements published on the Cathay careers page at the time of writing.

RequirementDetail
Right to live and work Right to live and work in Hong Kong or the Chinese Mainland. Candidates eligible for IANG or TTPS are also welcome to apply.
Age 18 or above.
Education Graduated from secondary school with good passes in English, and in Mathematics or Science. A university degree or an aviation major is an advantage.
English certificate A valid English proficiency certificate — see the table below.
Medical Physically fit and qualified for a Civil Aviation Department (HKCAD) Class 1 Medical Certificate.
Flight deck reach Able to meet flight deck reach requirements. Candidates may be asked to complete a static reach test and a flight simulator reach assessment.
ICAO English ICAO English Language Proficiency level 4 or above.
Background check Successful pre-employment background check, including criminal records.
Drug test The role is safety-sensitive: pre-employment drug testing under HKCAD CAD373, Annex B.

English language requirements

IELTS (Academic or General) 6.0 or above Listening 6.0+, Speaking 6.0+ Certificate must be valid at the time of application
TOEIC 800 or above Listening 400+, Speaking 160+ Both listening and speaking scores required
TOEFL iBT 80 or above Listening 21+, Speaking 21+ Tests taken before 21 January 2026
TOEFL iBT (new scale) 4.5 or above Listening 4.5+, Speaking 4.5+ Tests taken after 21 January 2026

A good overall score is not enough on its own — the Listening and Speaking minimums are separate requirements.

Selection process

  1. Online application — CV and details submitted through the careers site.
  2. ICAO English test — listening and speaking in an aviation context.
  3. Computer-based assessment — multi-tasking, spatial reasoning, reaction speed, numerical and logical reasoning.
  4. Interviews — motivation, understanding of the industry, judgement, teamwork.
  5. Group exercise — a task run in English, assessed on how the group reaches a conclusion.
  6. Medical examination — HKCAD Class 1, together with the pre-employment checks above.

What I prepare for each step

StepPreparation
Online application One-page CV built around discipline, teamwork and English communication. Every claim should survive a follow-up question.
ICAO English test A 90-second spoken answer recorded and played back, daily. ATC listening practice from live feeds.
Computer-based assessment Timed practice on multi-tasking and spatial rotation. Train the mix, not one question type.
Interviews Three question groups: why Cathay, why now, and what went wrong before and what changed.
Group exercise Practise summarising where the group has got to, instead of adding more opinions.
Medical Book the HKCAD Class 1 early. It is the slowest item and the least negotiable one.

Checklist

  • Confirm right to live and work in Hong Kong or the Chinese Mainland (or IANG / TTPS eligibility).
  • Collect secondary school certificates and transcripts, with English versions if needed.
  • Sit IELTS and reach 6.0 overall with Listening and Speaking at 6.0 or above.
  • Book an HKCAD Class 1 medical examination with an approved examiner.
  • Check vision, colour vision and hearing in advance.
  • Passport valid for at least 12 months.
  • Write a one-page English CV.
  • Understand the static reach test and the simulator reach assessment.
  • Practise computer-based assessment question types.
  • Prepare a two-minute spoken introduction.
  • Run one practice group discussion in English.
  • Submit the online application and record the application number and date.

Notes

  • The careers page publishes no minimum height figure; it uses flight deck reach requirements instead. Ignore old numbers circulating in forums.
  • A PPL is not required before applying. Self-funded flying helps your understanding but is not a selection criterion.
  • English is the one requirement that runs through every stage of the process.
  • Sponsored training usually comes with contractual conditions. Read the service period and termination clauses before signing.
